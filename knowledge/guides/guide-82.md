# $botCount guide

> Community guide for `$botCount` (function) — package **ForgeScript**. Approved 2025-06-15. [View on docs.botforge.org](https://docs.botforge.org/guide/guide-82)

**$botCount** counts the amount of bots **for all the guilds (servers) your bot is in.**
If you want to count the amount of bots of a specific guild, you'd use **[$guildBotCount](https://docs.botforge.org/function/$guildBotCount)** instead.
